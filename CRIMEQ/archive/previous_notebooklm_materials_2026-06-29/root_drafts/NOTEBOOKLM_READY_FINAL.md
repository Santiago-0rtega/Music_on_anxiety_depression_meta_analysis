## 📊 CODEBOOK STRUCTURE (CRIME-Q_Detailed_Codebook_v3.csv)

Each of the 20 items has these fields:

| Field | Content | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Item_ID** | 1X, 3X, 5Y, etc. | 5X |
| **Item_Name** | Full name | Music Intervention: Reporting |
| **Construct** | QoR, MQ, or RoB | QoR |
| **Question** | Assessment question | Is the music intervention described sufficiently for replication? |
| **Response_Options** | Possible answers | Yes; Partly; No |
| **Yes_Criteria** | What warrants "Yes" (detailed) | All core elements reported: music type/genre, delivery method, duration per exposure, frequency, timing, volume/intensity, control condition, individual vs. group |
| **Partly_Criteria** | What warrants "Partly" (detailed) | One or more core elements missing or vaguely stated |
| **No_Criteria** | What warrants "No" (detailed) | Music-vs-control insufficiently described. Cannot identify music or control condition |
| **Music_Examples** | Specific examples from music studies | Yes: "Classical piano (Chopin Nocturnes) via ceiling speakers at 70 dB, 30 min/day for 14 days...". Partly: "Classical music 30 min/day; control silence" - volume/specific pieces not stated. |
| **Common_Pitfalls** | What to watch out for | List EACH core element. Genre must be specific enough to replicate (Mozart < Mozart Sonata K545). Control condition MUST be explicit. |
