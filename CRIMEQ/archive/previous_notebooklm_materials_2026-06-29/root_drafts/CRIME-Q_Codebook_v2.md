# CRIME-Q Codebook for Music Studies (20 Items)

## Item 1: 1X - Peer review

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Peer review status?

**Criteria**: Yes=peer-reviewed journal | No=preprint/thesis | Partly=N/A | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 2: 3X - Animals reporting

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Animals sufficiently described?

**Criteria**: Yes=species, strain, sex, age, baseline | Partly=missing 1-2 descriptors | No=generic/insufficient | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 3: 3Y - Animals technical quality

**Construct**: MQ

**Question**: Characteristics comparable?

**Criteria**: Yes=group balance or matching | Partly=likely but incomplete | No=imbalance unaddressed | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 4: 3Z - Selection bias baseline

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Baseline balanced?

**Criteria**: Yes=arm-specific data AND balanced OR matching | No=imbalance unaddressed | Unclear=no baseline info | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 5: 4Y - Sample size

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Sample-size calculation?

**Criteria**: Yes=power analysis with effect size, power, alpha | Partly=mentioned but incomplete | No=none reported | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 6: 5X - Music reporting

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Music intervention described?

**Criteria**: Yes=genre, delivery, duration, frequency, timing, volume, control | Partly=one or more missing | No=insufficient | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 7: 5Y - Music technical quality

**Construct**: MQ

**Question**: Music design sound?

**Criteria**: Yes=distinct music/control; no cross-contamination | Partly=plausible but incomplete | No=clear problem | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 8: 5Z(1) - Randomization

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Allocation randomized?

**Criteria**: Yes=described random method (RNG, table, lots) | No=non-random | Unclear=no method stated | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 9: 5Z(2) - Random housing

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Housing randomized?

**Criteria**: Yes=random placement/counterbalancing/rotation | No=systematic placement confounded | Unclear=not reported | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 10: 5Z(3) - Outcome assessment

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Assessment unbiased?

**Criteria**: Yes=all tested at pre-specified time OR rule | No=outcome-dependent selection | Unclear=not reported | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 11: 6X - Ethics compliance

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Ethical approval reported?

**Criteria**: Yes=ethics/IACUC approval explicit | Partly=general statement, incomplete | No=none | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 12: 7X - Blinding reporting

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Blinding described?

**Criteria**: Yes=states WHO, WHEN, WHICH condition | Partly=unqualified or limited detail | No=no blinding | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 13: 7Z(1) - Experimenter blinding

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Experimenter blinded?

**Criteria**: Yes=procedures prevented knowing (coded, automated) | No=unblinded with influence | Unclear=not reported | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 14: 7Z(2) - Assessor blinding

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Assessor blinded?

**Criteria**: Yes=assessors/coders blinded or data masked | No=unblinded with judgment outcome | Unclear=not reported | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 15: 8X - Methods-results alignment

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Methods/results aligned?

**Criteria**: Yes=outcomes/labels/n/timing match | Partly=minor discrepancies, primary interpretable | No=major mismatches | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 16: 8Z(1) - Attrition bias

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Attrition handled?

**Criteria**: Yes=numbers allocated/analyzed/excluded clear | No=unequal or outcome-related | Unclear=insufficiently reported | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 17: 8Z(2) - Selective reporting

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Outcomes fully reported?

**Criteria**: Yes=pre-registration matches OR methods/results aligned | No=key outcome missing | Unclear=no protocol | Partly=N/A

---

## Item 18: 9X - Limitations

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: Limitations discussed?

**Criteria**: Yes=substantive limitations (design, sample, music) | Partly=generic/brief | No=none | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 19: 10X - Conflict-of-interest

**Construct**: QoR

**Question**: COI statement included?

**Criteria**: Yes=formal statement (including no conflicts) | No=no statement | Partly=N/A | Unclear=N/A

---

## Item 20: 10Z - Funder influence

**Construct**: RoB

**Question**: Free from funder bias?

**Criteria**: Yes=no conflict AND no inappropriate role | No=funder controlled design/reporting | Unclear=conflict absent | Partly=N/A

---
